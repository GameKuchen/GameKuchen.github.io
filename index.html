V. Incident Report: Biological Contamination Event #65-992x

[WARNING: RESTRICTED LORE DATABASE ENTRY #65-992X]

The following file has some really sensitive documents about body changing events that involve SCP-953 genetic material. You should read this at your own risk.

1. Preliminary Containment Breach Sequence

Well it all started at about 03:14 in the morning. We had this thing called Sub-Subject E-953-B in containment. It was basically a weaponized sickness made from the tissue samples of SCP-953. Anyway the containment broke down because Sector-G had a really big power failure. Sgt. Waldenstein went down there with Security Response Team Lambda-4 to lock the doors physically. When he was trying to manually close the main blast door a tiny crack in the glass window broke open. A huge spray of weird bio organic mist shot right out into the front room.

The base had automatic lockdown rules so the security doors sealed up right away. This meant Sgt. Waldenstein was locked inside the dirty room with the active sickness and no one could get to him.

2. Physical Transformation Logs (Mitotic Progression)

The mist ended up acting like a super fast mutation virus that carried SCP-953 DNA. If you look at the audio logs and cameras from the 14 hours he was stuck in there you can see what happened to his body. It was a gradual thing but it sure led to an interesting turn of events.

  • T+02h 15m: He started saying his nerves hurt really bad and his bones were aching all over the place. Scans later showed his tailbone starting to change and shift around.
  • T+05h 40m: His lower spine got a lot longer and it was really easy to see. The skin broke open and a new tail bone popped out. It grew muscles and that thick white fur that SCP-953 has pretty fast. The tail got to be about 65 centimeters long in total.
  • T+08h 12m: Then his head started changing. He got some really bad skull fractures because his normal human ears started shrinking away. The ear cartilage just kind of died and his body absorbed it completely. There were no traces left of his old ears.
  • T+11h 30m: Two furry animal ears started growing on top of his head. They poked right through his skin and hair. These new ears could move around a lot and he could hear really high pitched noises with them.
  • T+14h 00m: The whole change finally finished up. His vitals went back to normal. Usually when SCP-953 attacks someone they get really messed up but his body just naturally grew the new fox parts because his cells were dividing so fast from the trauma.

3. Medical and Psychological Evaluation

Even though the whole thing hurt a lot Sgt. Waldenstein kept his mind totally normal. He is still fully loyal to the Foundation if you would believe it. He did not get all crazy and homicidal like SCP-953 usually makes people. The doctors tested him after and the new parts are totally wired into his brain and bones. They gave him some Class A amnestics so he would not remember how bad the pain was and to stop phantom limb stuff. He got cleared to go back to work with some new rules. The people in charge of the site told everyone to just ignore the new fox parts during normal security shifts and treat him like usual.

4. Anomalous Trait Integration and Tactical Analysis

Getting the SCP-953 DNA mixed into him did more than just change how he looks. The new fox ears on his head actually let Sgt. Waldenstein hear way better than a normal person. He can hear things from 45 all the way up to 65000 Hertz. This is really helpful when he has to do containment stuff down in the underground levels because he can hear things coming from far away. The new tail also acts like a big balance thing for him. It really helps him move faster and keep his balance when he is in a stressful fight.

The biology guys at the site noticed he needs to eat a lot more protein now to keep up with the new body parts. So they had to change his normal food rations to give him extra meat and stuff to keep him healthy.

5. Site Inspectorate Command and Asset Classification

The Ethics Committee really argued about keeping Sgt. Waldenstein on the active duty roster. Usually the rules say you have to terminate or lock up anyone who gets mutated by weird biology stuff. But since he completely took in the SCP-953 DNA without losing his mind it was a really good chance to have a tactical edge over the anomalies.

The Site Inspectorate decided to step in and give him a special pass to keep working. Sgt. Waldenstein is now officially called an Anomalous Asset. They let him wear custom tactical gear that fits around his tail and ears. He has to take regular psych tests now though. If he starts acting aggressive like a polymorphic entity or if he stops being loyal they will terminate him right away.
