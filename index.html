V. Incident Report: Biological Contamination Event #65-992x

[WARNING: RESTRICTED LORE DATABASE ENTRY #65-992X]

The following file contains restricted documentation regarding physical changes involving SCP-953 genetic material. You should only proceed if you have the proper clearance.

1. Preliminary Containment Breach Sequence

At around 03:14 hours we had a breach involving Sub-Subject E-953-B. This was a weaponized sickness derived from SCP-953 tissue samples. Containment failed because of a massive power outage in Sector-G. Sgt. Waldenstein and Security Response Team Lambda-4 went down to establish a physical lock on the doors. While he was trying to manually close the main blast door a small crack in the glass window broke open. That released a heavy spray of the bio-organic vapor straight into the front room.

The base went into automated lockdown right then and the security doors sealed. This left Sgt. Waldenstein locked inside the contaminated room with the active anomaly.

2. Physical Transformation Logs (Mitotic Progression)

The vapor acted as a fast-acting mutagen that carried SCP-953 DNA. Well mixing an anomalous sickness with standard human biology sure led to an interesting turn of events. Analysis of the audio logs and internal cameras over the 14 hours he was locked in there captured how his body changed.

  • T+02h 15m: The subject reported severe nerve pain and his bones were aching constantly. Scans later showed that his tailbone was starting to restructure itself.
  • T+05h 40m: His lower spine got noticeably longer. The skin broke open as a new tailbone pushed through. It quickly grew muscles and the thick white-tipped fur that you normally see on SCP-953. This tail grew to a total length of roughly 65 centimeters.
  • T+08h 12m: Then the changes to his head started. He suffered some bad internal skull fractures because his normal human ears were shrinking. The ear cartilage just died off and his body completely absorbed it. After that phase there were no traces left of his original ears.
  • T+11h 30m: Two furry animal ears started developing on the top of his head. They grew right through his skin and hair. These new ears were fully mobile and he could hear high-frequency noises with them.
  • T+14h 00m: The transformation finally stopped and his core vitals stabilized. Usually when people are attacked by SCP-953 they suffer a lot of cellular damage. In this case his body just naturally synthesized the new fox parts through rapid cell division caused by the trauma.

3. Medical and Psychological Evaluation

Even with the severe pain of the transformation Sgt. Waldenstein kept his full mental capacity. He is still completely loyal to the Foundation. He did not show any of the homicidal urges that usually happen with SCP-953. Hence I guess the anomalous traits infused within him after the transformation did not affect his brain chemistry. The doctors tested him afterward and confirmed that the new parts are fully wired into his nervous system and skeleton. They gave him a targeted Class-A amnestic so he would not remember the pain of the transformation and to prevent phantom limb syndrome. Sgt. Waldenstein was cleared to return to active duty with some modified physical rules. The staff running the site instructed everyone to just ignore his new structural changes during normal security operations.

4. Anomalous Trait Integration and Tactical Analysis

Getting the SCP-953 genetics mixed into him led to some distinct physical changes beyond just how he looks. Those new fox ears actually give Sgt. Waldenstein much better hearing than a normal human. He can detect frequencies ranging from 45 to 65000 Hertz. This gives him a really distinct tactical advantage when he is doing containment work in the subterranean levels. The new tail also works as a secondary balance organ. It improves his agility and spatial awareness a lot during high-stress combat situations.

Well the Foundation biologists also noted that he needs a special high-protein diet now to maintain the extra metabolic load of the new body parts. His standard issue rations were modified to account for this.

5. Site Inspectorate Command and Asset Classification

The Ethics Committee heavily debated keeping Sgt. Waldenstein on active duty. Standard protocol usually says you have to terminate or permanently lock up personnel who get mutated by anomalous biological agents. However the fact that he completely absorbed the SCP-953 genetic material without losing his mind was a very unique tactical opportunity.

The Site Inspectorate decided to grant a special exemption. Sgt. Waldenstein is now officially classified as an Anomalous Asset. He is allowed to wear custom-modified tactical gear so his new physical alterations fit comfortably. He has to undergo regular psychological evaluations. If he shows any signs of aggressive polymorphic behavior or if his loyalty is compromised they will terminate him immediately.
